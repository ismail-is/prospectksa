# Prospect KSA

> Leading provider of heavy equipment rental, Aramco-certified manpower solutions, industrial blasting & coating, and lifting/rigging materials across Saudi Arabia.

## Overview

Prospect KSA (Ihtimal Company for General Contracting) is a premier industrial services provider based in Al-Khobar, Saudi Arabia. We serve major sectors including oil & gas, petrochemicals, energy, and construction with specialized solutions, high-capacity equipment, and certified professionals.

---

## Core Services

### 1. Equipment Rental
We offer a premium, modern fleet of heavy machinery and industrial equipment:
- **Lifting & Cranes:** Mobile cranes (50T to 500T), crawler cranes, and rough-terrain cranes.
- **Material Handling:** Forklifts (3T to 16T), telehandlers, and boom lifts.
- **Earthmoving:** Excavators, loaders, dump trucks, and graders.
- **Power & Air:** Air compressors, diesel generators (50kVA to 1000kVA), and light towers.

### 2. Manpower Services
Certified skilled workforce solutions tailored to heavy industrial projects:
- **Aramco-Certified Personnel:** Riggers (Level I, II, III), safety officers, and QA/QC inspectors.
- **Engineering & Supervision:** Mechanical, electrical, civil, and planning engineers.
- **Technical Trades:** Pipefitters, welders (multi-process), fabricators, instrument technicians, and heavy equipment operators.

### 3. Blasting & Coating
Industrial surface preparation and protection services:
- **Surface Preparation:** Dry abrasive blasting, grit blasting, sandblasting, and water jetting.
- **Coating & Lining:** Epoxy coatings, polyurethane coatings, high-temperature linings, and fireproofing.
- **QA/QC:** NACE-certified inspectors ensuring coating thickness and adhesion compliance.

### 4. Lifting Materials
Certified rigging hardware and lifting accessories:
- **Hardware:** Shackles, turnbuckles, hooks, wire rope clips, and master links.
- **Slings:** Wire rope slings, polyester webbing slings, and chain slings.
- **Inspection & Testing:** Third-party certification and load testing services.
